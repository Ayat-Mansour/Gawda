Navigating the Path

NAVIGATING THE PATHA story of resilience and actionable advice on overcoming cyberbullying

A STORY OF LIGHT

Leo felt isolated when hurtful comments appeared on his phone. Instead of reacting with anger, he paused, regained his composure, and remembered that he could control how he responded.

CORRECT ACTIONS TO TAKE

  1. Secure evidence — save clear screenshots before messages are deleted
  2. Tell a trusted adult — a parent, teacher, or counselor
  3. Block and report — use the platform tools immediately

Student’s Guide to Handling Bullying

STUDENT’S GUIDE TO HANDLING BULLYINGPRACTICAL STEPS
ARE YOU BEING BULLIED?

STEP 1: DOCUMENT EVIDENCE 📷

  • RECORD DATES, TIMES, AND WITNESSES.
  • SAVE SCREENSHOTS OR MESSAGES.

STEP 2: REPORT TO SOMEONE YOU TRUST 🤝

  • TALK TO YOUR PARENTS, TEACHER, OR STUDENT COUNSELOR.
  • EXPLAIN WHAT IS HAPPENING CLEARLY.

STEP 3: TAKE IMMEDIATE & SAFE ACTIONS 🛡️

  • AVOID DIRECT CONFRONTATION IF UNSAFE.
  • ASK FOR HELP WHEN NEEDED.
  • IGNORE THE BULLIES IF POSSIBLE.

STEP 4: FOLLOW-UP & SUPPORT ⚖️

  • CONFIRM THE SCHOOL HAS TAKEN ACTION.
  • TALK ABOUT YOUR FEELINGS FOR PSYCHOLOGICAL SUPPORT.

    Digital Respect Pledge

    I promise to:

    1. Be kind and constructive in what I post.
    2. Respect privacy: think before I share personal information, and never tag people without permission.
    3. Use credible sources and fact-check before I share.
    4. Think before posting, because my digital footprint is permanent.
    5. Report cyberbullying to a trusted adult and support those who are hurt.
    6. Never post offensive content, hate speech, or discrimination.

    Your name stays on this screen only. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
